# Visual assets

The website uses custom, illustrative Islamic-learning images rather than unverified staff photographs or unrelated external stock photos.

- Images are stored locally under `public/images/custom/`.
- They were optimized into WebP format for fast page loads.
- `lib/siteImages.js` references these assets.
- Some illustrations may be reused across pages; course cards on the reference-style homepage are intentionally typography-driven rather than large photographs.
- Teacher images are **illustrative only**, not verified photographs of actual staff members. Replace those with authorized real staff photographs when available.
- Review any AI-generated Quran-like text visually before using it for religious teaching materials; do not treat image text as an accurate Quran copy.
